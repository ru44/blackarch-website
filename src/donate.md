# Help us today

BlackArch Linux is entirely free and open source. The team members work on it voluntarily, in their free time, despite the large amount of time it takes every day.
Another thing this project requires to keep moving forward is donations. For instance, the hosting of the website, domain name, download mirrors, testing hardware... isn't free.

Every donation, even the most modest, is a great help and will be used only to pay for BlackArch expenses, and fund the future development of the project.
We appreciate your interest in this project as we continue to develop BlackArch Linux.

There are many ways to help us. Money is of course a must. But, if you can't give a financial contribution, you may help in another way.
For example: translating, offering support to users in need (in Matrix), or anything that can help improve BlackArch. Also, a simple star on the BlackArch repository on GitHub goes a long way... :D

We also welcome any organization/company interested in becoming an official sponsor of BlackArch Linux.
You can contact us by Matrix or by email.
